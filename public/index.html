How the Installer World Cup works

Weeks 1-4 — League Play (Aug 10 – Sep 6)

Every week runs Sunday to Saturday and stands alone — revenue resets each week. Top revenue team of the week earns 3 points, second highest earns 1 point, everyone else earns 0. Points add up across all 4 weeks. Tied on total points at the end? Highest combined revenue across the 4 weeks wins the tiebreaker.

Week 5 — The Knockout Finale (Sep 7 – Sep 13)

The two teams with the most league points go head to head. Week 5 is scored fresh — whoever installs the most revenue that week alone wins the trip. League points don't carry into the Finale result, they only decided who's in it.

Who's competing

Electrician, Solo — full contract value counts to you, for the electrical work you touched.
Install Crew Only — full contract value counts to your crew, on the jobs you installed.
Combined Team (Electrician + Install) — one team, full job value counts once, includes full 3-man electrical plus install crews.
If a solo electrician and a separately-registered crew both work the same job, each is credited the full value on their own board. Rosters locked Aug 10 — no swaps once the season started.

What counts as revenue

Full contract value, only when the job is actually done — meaning it's cleared all four gates: Ops Checklist submitted fully and completely, POWRA completed before work starts on site, Method Statement followed, and System Commissioned — live and reporting correctly in the monitoring app. Miss one and the job doesn't count yet, not partially. It counts in whichever week it actually clears all four.

The prize

Port Renfrew. Cabin. Boat on the water. The winning team goes together, three of you — whether that's one combined crew, or an electrician and a two-man install crew who topped the board side by side, however you get there, it's a three-man trip. Spouses welcome where applicable. Exact dates get locked in with the winning team after Week 5.
